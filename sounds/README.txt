Put your mp3 files in this folder. A missing file keeps the built-in synth sound.

  strike.mp3  - striker hits a coin        (alias: shot.mp3)
  coin.mp3    - coin hits coin             (alias: ball.mp3)
  wall.mp3    - hits the cushion           (alias: cushion.mp3)
  pocket.mp3  - coin / striker drops in a pocket
  foul.mp3    - striker pocketed (foul)    (alias: wrong.mp3)
  win.mp3     - game over                  (alias: gameover.mp3)
  tick.mp3    - tick while sliding the striker

Keep them short (under half a second, except win.mp3).
